LAMENTING MALICE by Cynthoni (Album Review)

by 1111nemo | 2026-09-09

If you don't have the time to read this review, I'll give you THE BOTTOM LINE:
"Sewerslvt is hate targeted within. Cythoni's LAMENTING MALICE is repressed rage targeted towards reality itself."

LAMENTING MALICE was originally released on December 4th, 2025 as a Bandcamp/Patreon exclusive release. But after half a year, and I quote "Begrudgingly", they decided to release it on other platforms in 128kbps to prevent re-uploads. And honestly, I f--k with that. Everyone should universally act like Spotify never existed in the first place. Ironically though, I found this tape while I was perusing for new music on YouTube. And again, begrudgingly, I still use Apple Music.

Moving on...

The last time I closely followed their music was when they decided to "rebrand" themselves from "Sewerslvt" to "Cynthoni". And both of them carry a different perspective on the same truth.

LAMENTING DESPAIR

Depression isn't a 'one-note' feeling. It can turn itself into many forms of emotions. It's a spectrum between complete helplessness and seeing light at the end of the tunnel. And you can feel differently depending on the day. Sewerslvt portrayed that excellently. The controlled chaos of the drums, the droning ambiance, the rather nostalgic synths, they all help in making you feel every nuance of depression.

I relate to their music, and maybe a little too much. And when they decided to change their name (it was around 2021 I think), that's when I decided to surround myself with things that can better myself, rather than things that can help me lament my despair.

LAMENTING REALITY

As I said, after several years of not following their music, I found their music thanks to the YouTube algorithm. They said it in the descriptions of the video that they released this album on streaming platforms "begrudgingly". I laughed a bit, and I completely understand.

I was shocked when I listened to it several minutes in. I got severely hooked.

A stark contrast to Sewerslvt. You can infer the direction of the album from the narration of the first track. On track 2, I can feel that the mix got harsher. On track 3, I feel that the drums have gotten even more chaotic, violent even. The bold 808s and the synths convey a deep, repressed rage.

And yet again, I can feel myself relating to them again. But not them as Sewerslvt. This time, them as Cynthoni.

Between the wars, the complete misuse of AI, the CEOs, the algorithms, the doomscrolls, the petty turned violent arguments on social media, everything... they all have gotten tiring. I completely understand why a lot of people started romanticizing the analogs, the decentralizations, the "disconnect from social media" movements. The sheer flow of information we get on the internet is tiring us out. We don't even have time to lament ourselves anymore. The reality itself is tiring us out. And we want a way to escape from it. LAMENTING MALICE is all that, and several more.

Sewerslvt is hate targeted within. Cythoni's LAMENTING MALICE is repressed rage targeted towards reality itself.
